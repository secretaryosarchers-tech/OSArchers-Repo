Who We Are

A Club Built on Community & Passion

Founded in April 2019, OS Archers started with a simple goal: to make archery genuinely accessible to everyone. We've since grown into a vibrant, welcoming club for archers of all ages and abilities.

We are affiliated with Archery GB — the national governing body for the sport — alongside regional and county affiliations with SCAS and HAA. Safety, inclusivity, and enjoyment are at the heart of everything we do.

Our sessions are held at our ground in Nursling, Southampton, with experienced, qualified coaches always on hand to help you improve.

What We Shoot

All Disciplines Welcome

Whether you prefer the elegance of a recurve or the tradition of a longbow, there's a place for every archer at OS Archers.

01

Recurve

The classic Olympic discipline. Technical, elegant, and deeply rewarding — the perfect starting point for most new archers.

02

Compound

Precision engineering meets athleticism. Compound bows offer exceptional accuracy and reward consistent, disciplined practice.

03

Longbow

Connect with archery's historic roots. A pure test of skill, instinct, and the timeless bond between archer and bow.

04

Barebow

Stripped back to essentials — no sights, no stabilisers. Barebow demands heightened technique and a refined sense of feel.
