Governing Body Fees

Additional Affiliation Fees

As an affiliated club, membership of OS Archers connects you to the national and regional governing bodies for archery. Here's what's included in your club fee and what you need to arrange separately.

SCAS Included

Southern Counties Archery Society

SCAS affiliation is included in your OS Archers club membership fee. No separate payment is required.

Visit SCAS → SCAS Fees →
HAA Included

Hampshire Archery Association

HAA affiliation is included in your OS Archers club membership fee. No separate payment is required.

Visit HAA → HAA Fees →
AGB Paid Separately

Archery GB

AGB membership is not included in the OS Archers club fee. Each member must arrange and pay their AGB membership individually via the Sport 80 portal. AGB membership is required to shoot at affiliated clubs and competitions.

Visit AGB → Pay via Sport 80 → AGB Membership Info →
WA Via AGB

World Archery

World Archery is the international governing body for the sport. As an AGB member you are automatically connected to World Archery — no separate fee applies at club level.

Visit World Archery →
Renewing & Joining

How to Pay Your Fees

Club fees are collected by the secretary and renewed on your individual anniversary date. AGB fees are managed separately through the Sport 80 portal — you are responsible for keeping your own AGB membership current.

If you're new to the club, speak to the secretary after completing a beginners course or get in touch by email to find out about joining.

1
Pay Your Club Fee Contact the secretary to arrange payment of your OS Archers annual club fee (£40 OS / £50 non-OS). This covers SCAS and HAA affiliation.
2
Pay Your AGB Fee via Sport 80 Log in to Sport 80 at agb.sport80.com and renew your Archery GB membership. Check the AGB website for current individual rates.
3
Keep Your Records Up to Date Once renewed, make sure your details are current on Golden Records Online so your scores and classifications continue to be tracked correctly.
4
Any Questions? Contact the secretary at secretary@osarchers.co.uk if you have any questions about your membership or fees.
