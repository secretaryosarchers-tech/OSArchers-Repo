Members Guide

Logging Your Scores

A step-by-step guide to logging your scores on Golden Records Online — the system that powers our club records, handicaps, and classifications.

Open Golden Records → View the Guide ↓
Why Log Your Scores

Every Arrow Counts

Golden Records Online is the club's scoring platform, provided by Archery Club Management. Every score you log goes towards your personal handicap, your classifications, and the club records displayed on the OS Archers website.

You should have received your login details by email when you joined the club. If you haven't received them or have lost them, contact the secretary and we'll get you set up.

Scores must be accompanied by a photo of your score sheet or a screenshot from a scoring app — this is required for the score to count towards records and classifications.

📊

Your Handicap

Logged scores build your handicap, used to level the playing field in club competitions.

🏅

Classifications

Progress through AGB outdoor and indoor classifications from Archer 3rd Class to Bowman 1st Class.

🏆

Club Records

Your best scores feed the live club records table on the OS Archers website.

📈

Track Progress

See your scoring history over time and watch your game improve round by round.

Step by Step

How to Log a Score

Follow these steps after each round to keep your records up to date.

1

Log in to Golden Records Online

Go to archery-records.net and sign in with the credentials you received by email when you joined the club.

ℹ️ Can't find your login details? Email the secretary at secretary@osarchers.co.uk and we'll resend them.
2

View Your Existing Scores

Once logged in you'll see your scoring dashboard showing any rounds you've already submitted. This is your personal score history.

📱 The layout looks slightly different on mobile versus desktop — both work the same way, just with a different arrangement of controls.
3

Add a New Score

To add a new score, click the Add My Score button — on desktop this appears with a + symbol to its left. On mobile, tap the menu icon in the top right of the screen, slide the screen to the left, then tap the + symbol.

Fill in the round details: date, age group, bow class, round, score, hits, golds, and Xs. All fields should match your score sheet exactly. Set Where Shot to "OS Field" for club sessions.

📸 Important: Use Add Up To 4 Scoresheets or Results at the bottom to attach a photo of your score sheet. Accepted file types are .jpg, .gif, .png, .pdf, and .heic. Scores without evidence cannot count towards records or classifications.
4

Submit Your Score

Once you've filled in all the details and attached your score sheet photo, click Submit. Your score will be reviewed and, if it's a personal best for that round, it will automatically update the club records.

✅ Try to submit scores promptly after shooting — ideally the same day. This keeps the club records and handicaps up to date for everyone.
Device Guide

Desktop vs Mobile

Golden Records Online works on both desktop and mobile, but the layout varies. Here's what to expect on each.

💻

Desktop & Laptop

On a desktop or laptop the full navigation is visible. The Add My Score button with a + symbol appears prominently in the main interface.

All columns and controls are visible without scrolling, making it the easiest way to review your score history and add new rounds.

📱

Mobile & Tablet

On mobile the layout is condensed. Tap the menu icon in the top right of the screen, then slide the screen to the left to reveal the controls — or tap the + symbol directly.

The form fields work the same way as desktop. Make sure you have your score sheet photo ready to attach before you begin.
