What to Expect

Two Challenges. Enter One, the Other, or Both.

The Imperial & Metric Challenge is actually two separate challenges — the historic Imperial rounds our club is known for, and the precision of Metric distances. Sign up to whichever suits you, or take on both. Cost is still to be determined — full details, entry information, and rules will be published closer to launch.

The Imperial Challenge

Traditional yardage distances, honouring the format that inspired the original Imperial Challenge.

The Metric Challenge

Modern metric distances, bringing a fresh test of consistency and accuracy for every bow class.
