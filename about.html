OS Archers

About Our Club

Founded in April 2019, OS Archers is a family-friendly, Archery GB affiliated club based in Nursling, Southampton — open to all ages, disciplines, and abilities.

Who We Are

A Club Built on Community & Passion

Founded in April 2019, OS Archers started with a simple goal: to make archery genuinely accessible to everyone. We've since grown into a vibrant, welcoming club for archers of all ages and abilities.

Our members come from all backgrounds and levels of expertise. Some have been practising for years, some are returning after a break, and some are complete beginners finding their feet for the first time.

We are affiliated with Archery GB — the national governing body for the sport — alongside regional and county affiliations with SCAS and HAA. Safety, inclusivity, and enjoyment are at the heart of everything we do.

We offer coaching from qualified instructors at Level 1 and Level 2, and actively encourage and support new coaches by partially covering their training costs.

Our Mission

Archery for Everyone

At our club, we celebrate all forms of archery. Whether your passion is Compound, Recurve, Barebow, or Traditional shooting, you'll find a place where every discipline is valued and respected.

We are committed to providing a safe, supportive environment where archers of all experience levels can learn, grow, and enjoy the sport. Our sessions are overseen by trained Field Captains and qualified coaches to ensure safety, guidance, and great shooting experiences.

Archery is a sport for everyone — from age 10 to 100. Whether you're shooting for fun, aiming for excellence, or embracing archery as a lifelong activity, we're here to support your journey.

To keep our club thriving, we focus on building a strong coaching team, including Level 1 and Level 2 Coaches, and creating opportunities to train and fund new coaches. We also depend on the dedication of our volunteers — from committee members to general helpers, our club runs smoothly thanks to those who give their time, skills, and enthusiasm.

8th AGM — March 2026

Year in Review

A summary of highlights and achievements from the 2025 club year, presented at the AGM on 24th March 2026, held via Teams with 11 members attending.

80+

New Club Records

More than 80 new club records were set across all levels in 2025 — more than double the 35 achieved the previous year, driven largely by the Imperial Challenge.

102

Imperial Challenge Rounds

11 OS Archers members and 3 from Merdon Castle took part in the Imperial Challenge, shooting 102 rounds in total. 4 achieved Bronze, 5 Silver, and 2 Gold.

15

Handicap Holders

15 members now hold registered handicaps. The AGB Handicap Improvement Medal was awarded to Jacqueline Marsh for the greatest improvement of the year.

22+

Tournaments Attended

OS Archers was represented at more than 22 tournaments in 2025. 6 members attended at least one external competition and 3 HAA records were claimed.

🎃

Halloween Shoot

24 archers attended the Halloween shoot, including 3 guests by invitation. An article was submitted to the AGB magazine for potential publication.

💰

Finances

The club recorded a small negative profit this year, attributable to planned investments: new beginner equipment, target maintenance, a new metal detector, and Imperial Challenge medals and badges. Net assets stand at £4,010.95.

Club Goals

Objectives for 2026

The committee agreed four goals for the coming year at the 8th AGM. These build on the strong performance in competitions and records during 2025, while addressing areas where the club can grow further.

Progress against these goals will be reviewed at the 9th AGM, scheduled for 23rd March 2027.

  • 1
    Grow Membership Increase active membership by at least 10 members. Beginners' courses are the primary route — members are encouraged to help promote and assist with these.
  • 2
    Develop Coaching Support at least one member towards a new coaching qualification at Level 1 or Level 2, continuing the club's commitment to funded coach training.
  • 3
    Run the Imperial Challenge Deliver the Imperial Challenge again in 2026, building on the outstanding participation and record-breaking performance seen in 2025. A new Metric Challenge has also been introduced this year.
  • 4
    Quarterly Bow Maintenance Sessions Schedule regular bow maintenance and tuning sessions on a quarterly basis, led by Keith Giles, to support member equipment care and knowledge.
