Club Bows

OS Archers maintains a collection of loaner bows available to members who are yet to purchase their own equipment. Each bow is named after a character from the Robin Hood legend — a nod to archery's most famous outlaw.

The Collection

Legendary Bows of Sherwood Forest

Our club bow collection consists of seven recurve bows, each named after a character from the Robin Hood legend. They are available for use by members who are yet to purchase their own equipment. Separate equipment is provided for those attending a beginners course.

All bows are recurve style, suitable for beginners. The collection covers a range of draw weights and lengths, with both right-handed and left-handed options available.

If you'd like to borrow a club bow for a session, speak to a Field Captain or coach on the day. All equipment is checked and maintained regularly.

Borrowing a Bow

How to Borrow a Club Bow

Club bows are available to use at OS Archers sessions free of charge. They're ideal for members who are yet to purchase their own equipment, or for those who want to try a different draw weight or orientation before committing to a purchase.

All bows are maintained and inspected regularly. If you notice any damage or issues with a club bow, please report it to a Field Captain or coach immediately.
