Our Commitment

Safeguarding at OS Archers

OS Archers adopts the Archery GB Policy for Safeguarding Children, Young People and Adults at Risk in full. This is a mandatory requirement of our AGB affiliation and applies to all members, coaches, volunteers, and visitors to the club.

We believe that the welfare of every member is paramount. Everyone who participates in archery at OS Archers — regardless of age, background, or ability — has the right to do so in a safe, happy, and welcoming environment, free from harm, abuse, and poor practice.

Our Club Welfare Officer is the first point of contact for any safeguarding concern within the club. All concerns are treated seriously, handled sensitively, and reported in line with AGB guidance.

The full AGB safeguarding policies, procedures, and guidance documents are available to download from the Archery GB website.

Our Approach

Safeguarding Principles

These principles underpin everything we do at OS Archers to keep our members safe and our club a welcoming place for all.

🛡️

Welfare is Paramount

The safety and welfare of every child, young person, and adult at risk always takes priority over all other considerations.

🤝

Everyone's Responsibility

Safeguarding is not just the Welfare Officer's job. Every member, coach, and volunteer shares responsibility for keeping our club safe.

🚫

We will not tolerate bullying, harassment, abuse, or discrimination of any kind — whether physical, verbal, or emotional.

📣

Anyone with a concern should report it. Doing nothing is never acceptable. Concerns can be raised confidentially and will always be taken seriously.

We are committed to an environment that embraces diversity and respects the rights of all members, regardless of age, background, or ability.

✅

Safe Recruitment

Taking Action

How to Report a Concern

If you have a concern about the welfare of a child, young person, or adult at risk — whether inside or outside the club — follow these steps. Remember: it is not your job to decide if abuse has taken place. Your job is to report your concern.

1

Is there immediate danger?

If you believe a child or adult is in immediate danger of harm, call 999 first. Do not wait to speak to the Welfare Officer. Advise the call handler that there is a child protection or adult at risk concern.

🚨 Always call 999 in an emergency. The Police or Social Services will advise on next steps. You must also report this to the Club Welfare Officer within 24 hours.
2

Contact the Club Welfare Officer

For all non-emergency concerns, contact our Club Welfare Officer, Joanne Davage, as soon as possible at secretary@osarchers.co.uk. You can also speak to her at any club session.

If the concern involves the Club Welfare Officer directly, or you feel a matter has not been dealt with adequately, contact the club Secretary to escalate.

ℹ️ All information shared will be treated as confidential and handled on a need-to-know basis in line with AGB guidance.
3

Report to Archery GB

Concerns can also be reported directly to the Archery GB safeguarding team at any time, either via their online reporting form or by email. The AGB National Lead Safeguarding Officer is Anne Rook.

Contact AGB safeguarding: safeguarding@archerygb.org

You can also submit a concern anonymously via the AGB online form:

Report a Concern to AGB →
4

Keep a Record

Make a written note of your concern as soon as possible — what you saw or heard, when, and who was involved. Use factual language and avoid interpretation. Pass this record to the Club Welfare Officer.

📋 Do not discuss the concern with other club members or share it on social media. Confidentiality protects everyone involved.

Safe Recruitment

DBS Checks & Vetting

OS Archers follows the AGB requirements for safe recruitment of coaches and volunteers. All coaches and volunteers working in regulated roles must hold a valid DBS check before working with children, young people, or adults at risk.

AGB licensed coaches are also required to complete tutor-led safeguarding training, renewed every three years, to ensure their knowledge remains current.

🔍
DBS Checks All coaches and volunteers in regulated roles must hold a valid Disclosure and Barring Service (DBS) check before working with vulnerable groups.
🎓
Safeguarding Training AGB licensed coaches must complete approved tutor-led safeguarding training and renew it every three years.
🪪
AGB Membership All coaches must hold a current AGB coaching licence. Parents and members can ask to see this at any time.
🤝
NSPCC CPSU Standards Archery GB has achieved Advanced Standards through the NSPCC Child Protection in Sport Unit, covering our affiliated clubs.
