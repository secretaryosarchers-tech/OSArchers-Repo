About the Challenge

The Imperial Challenge is structured to accommodate archers of all skill levels, from beginners to seasoned competitors. Each tier becomes increasingly challenging and at the end of the challenge archers will be awarded a medal for the highest tier they have completed (being six AGB rounds).

If you do not wish to complete a tier that's fine too as archers shooting a minimum of ANY 3 rounds across the 18 rounds available will receive an OSA participation badge.

Please note: This is a personal challenge not a competition, so scores will be accepted in good faith. If you subsequently achieve a higher score for a round, you can simply submit it on the interactive score sheet.
