The Role

What Is a Field Captain?

The Field Captain is responsible for overseeing the safe operation of every OS Archers session. This role is arranged through the WhatsApp group ahead of each session, and no shooting is permitted without a named Field Captain.

There must also be a minimum of two adults (18+) present for any session to run. Every member shares responsibility for range safety and for helping set up and pack away — the Field Captain coordinates this but it is a collective responsibility.

This guide covers everything from emailing OS security before arrival, through range setup, running the line, and ensuring all equipment is stored away correctly at the end.

Before & During the Session

Range Setup

Follow these steps in order before every session. Setup is a collective responsibility — direct members to help.

1

Email OS Security

Before the session, email OS security at SECURITY@OS.UK to notify them that the range will be in use. This should be done ahead of arrival.

ℹ️ If you need additional equipment from the dry store during the session, the Field Captain should collect the key from security. Always return the key before leaving the site.
2

Collect Equipment from the Shed

Obtain signage and all range equipment from the shed at point 2. Equipment is stored on the club's equipment trolley. The trolley contains:

Range warning signs · spare target pins · spare target faces · shooting line tape · waiting line rope · 100m range measuring tape · score sheets · spare pens

ℹ️ The dry store contains additional target faces and all beginner bows and accessories including sights, tabs, and bracers.
3

Place Warning Signs at Point 5

Place warning signs across the grass and path at point 5. You need 6 signs in total — 2 on the path and 4 on the grass.

Anchor grass signs using tent pegs through the hole at the base. Secure path signs using larger wooden stakes pushed down through the top. Stakes and pegs are on the equipment trolley.

⚠️ 6 signs are required at point 5 as this is the highest risk area — anyone walking along the path here is walking towards the arrows and is visible from the shooting line.
4

Set Up the Shooting Line

The shooting line uses a 30m tape measure from the equipment trolley, with a second rope for the equipment line if needed. Place this along the edge of the building at point 6 — it can be moved forward if the full range distance is not needed.

Place the trolley to block the path at point 6. Position the two additional signs — one for the path, one for the grass — next to the trolley to create a highly visible barrier.

👁️ Be vigilant for walkers throughout the session. OS security staff sometimes patrol the site.
5

Set Up the Targets

Move targets safely to the range and position them at the required distance. The wheeled foam targets must be secured to the ground using the peg and rope tie attached to the back of each target — particularly important in high winds.

If using straw targets on tripod stands, the straw targets must be secured to the ground every time using the tie backs and pegs from the equipment trolley. Use two target pins to secure the tie backs to the straw boss. Make sure the rope is taut before pegging to the ground. Tripods either have holes in the base for tent pegs, or wooden T-bars for tripods with screw eyes.

🚨 Straw targets must always be secured to the ground — even in calm conditions. Unsecured targets are a safety hazard.
6

Run the Session

Before giving the signal to shoot, take a good look around the entire range to check for anyone on or approaching the range. Only if the range is clear should you give the signal to shoot.

Keep the line moving quickly to maximise ends per session. Assist — or direct others to assist — with any issues such as loose targets, pinning, or lost arrows.

⚠️ Follow AGB guidance at all times and politely but firmly address any unsafe behaviour — for example, walking with a loaded bow or running on the range.
7

Pack Away & Close Down

At the end of the session, ensure all equipment is returned to the trolley and all targets are stored safely in the target storage area at point 3.

Straw targets should be covered with plastic sheeting. Push at least two wheeled foam targets against the straw targets to keep the sheeting in place. Tripod stands should be stored against the wall — please lay them flat.

Return the equipment trolley to the shed and, if the dry store key was used, return it to OS security before leaving.

🔑 Do not leave the site without returning the dry store key to security if it was used during the session.
Responsibilities

Field Captain Duties

A summary of the key responsibilities for any Field Captain running a session at the OS Archers range.

📋

Pre-Session Notification

Email OS security at SECURITY@OS.UK before the session to notify them the range will be in use.

⚠️

Signage Setup

Ensure all 6 warning signs are correctly placed at point 5 with tent pegs and stakes, plus 2 further signs at the shooting line.

🎯

Target Safety

Ensure all targets are moved safely to and from the range and secured to the ground — wheeled foam targets in high winds, straw targets every time.

🔊

Shooting Commands

Control the timing of shooting and collection using whistle or voice commands. Check the range is clear before every signal to shoot.

👀

Range Vigilance

Maintain awareness of walkers and OS security patrols throughout the session. Everyone is responsible, but the FC coordinates this.

🏹

AGB Safe Shooting

Enforce AGB safe shooting guidance and politely address any unsafe behaviour from archers during the session.

🛠️

Problem Resolution

Assist or direct others to resolve issues with targets or arrows during the session, including re-pinning loose targets and finding lost arrows.

🗄️

Close Down

Ensure all equipment is returned to the trolley and all targets are stored correctly. Return the dry store key to security if used.

Range Commands

Whistle & Voice Commands

These are the standard commands used to control the shooting line. They can be given by whistle or voice. All archers should be familiar with them.

1 blast

Shoot

One whistle blast or voice command signals that the range is clear and archers may begin shooting. Only given after a full visual check of the range.

3 blasts

Collect

Three whistle blasts or voice command signals that all archers have finished shooting and returned to the waiting line, and arrows may now be collected.

Fast / Stop

Cease Fire

The "Fast" or "Stop" commands are used to immediately halt all shooting in the event of a safety risk. Any archer may use this command — do not hesitate.

Distance Markers

Range Flag System

Distance markers are indicated by coloured flags placed in the rough grass to the side of the range. The shooting line is also marked by a red flag — all distances are measured from this point. A full 100m tape is kept in the equipment trolley for exact target placement.

🟡

Yellow Flags — Imperial (Yards)

Yellow flags mark the distances required to shoot official British competition rounds in yards, using five-zone scoring (9 to 1).

20 yds 30 yds 40 yds 50 yds 60 yds 80 yds 100 yds
🔴

Red Flags — Metric (Metres)

Red flags mark the distances needed for official World Archery (FITA) rounds in metres. The shooting line itself is also marked with a red flag.

30 m 50 m 60 m 70 m 90 m
