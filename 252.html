What Is the 252?

A Scheme for Every Member

The 252 Scheme is a structured progression badge scheme designed to give every archer at OS Archers a personal target to work towards, regardless of their current level.

Named after the Recurve score target of 252, the scheme runs from 20 yards through to 100 yards. The required score varies by bow style — Compound, Barebow, and Longbow archers each have their own qualifying totals. All bow styles are welcome.

Two qualifying scores at any given distance earns you the coloured badge for that distance. Scoresheets are available at the range or to download — fill in your 36 arrow values, get the sheet signed by a witness, then enter your score onto the club's records system.

Step by Step

How It Works

Four simple steps to earning your 252 badge at any distance.

01

Get a Scoresheet

Pick up a 252 scoresheet from the cart at the range, or download one using the link on this page. You'll need one sheet per qualifying attempt.

02

Shoot Your 36 Arrows

Shoot 36 arrows at the chosen distance using a 122cm target face. Fill in your arrow values on the scoresheet as you go. You need to score 252 or more.

03

Get It Witnessed

Ask another club member to witness and sign your scoresheet. Both your name and the witness's name must be on the sheet for the score to count.

04

Log Your Score

Enter your score on Golden Records Online and attach a photo of your signed scoresheet. Two qualifying scores at the same distance earns the badge.

The Badges

Distances & Scores Required

There are seven distances in the scheme, each with its own coloured badge. The score required varies by bow style — 252 is the Recurve target. All distances use a 122cm face and are shot over 6 sighters + 36 scoring arrows.
