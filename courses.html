About the Course

Everything You Need to Get Started

Our beginners course is the ideal way to take up archery safely and confidently. Delivered across two weekend mornings, it covers everything from handling a bow for the first time through to shooting independently on the range.

On completion you will be able to take up archery at any Archery GB registered club in the country — and of course we'd love you to join us at OS Archers.

Courses are led by our qualified Level 2 AGB coaches in a friendly, supportive environment. Numbers are kept small so everyone gets plenty of individual attention.

On the Day

What to Expect

Here's what happens during your two mornings with us.

01

Equipment

All equipment you need will be provided and set up ready for your arrival. Bow, arrows, finger tab, bracer, stand — everything is included. You'll learn what it all is and how to use it safely during the course.

02

The Range

The range is prepared and ready to go before you arrive. You'll have a target, normally shared with another course participant, and a 9:30am prompt start once everyone has arrived.

03

Safety First

Safety is covered thoroughly on day one. Our coaches will walk you through range commands, safe arrow handling, and the rules of the shooting line before anyone picks up a bow.

04

Shooting

By the end of day one you'll be shooting arrows downrange. Day two builds on technique, range etiquette, and prepares you for joining a club and shooting independently.

Be Prepared

What to Bring

We provide everything archery-related — all you need to bring is yourself and a few creature comforts for a morning outdoors.

Courses run outdoors on our Nursling ground, so dress appropriately for the weather. Closed-toe shoes are required on the range.

☕
Refreshments Drinks and food — the course runs for around three hours so bring a snack and something to drink.
👟
Suitable Footwear Closed-toe shoes or boots. No sandals or flip flops on the range.
🧥
Appropriate Clothing Dress for the weather — we shoot outdoors. Avoid loose sleeves which can catch the bowstring.
😊
Enthusiasm No prior experience needed — just a willingness to give it a go and have fun.
☀️
Nice Weather If you can manage it — though we shoot rain or shine!
